# Gridhunt for Home Assistant

Bring your Gridhunt rides into Home Assistant: a "riding" sensor, route progress,
new tiles and square size, your live position on the map, automations triggered
by ride events, and a Lovelace card.

Set up under Settings, Devices & services, Add integration, Gridhunt. Pair with a code
or paste a token created in Gridhunt (Settings, Connections, Home Assistant).
